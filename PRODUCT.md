# PRODUCT — J's Home Renovations & Relevels LLC

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS, hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (one-page site)

## Users
- Owners of mobile / manufactured homes whose home has settled (sticking doors, cracks, sloping floors), and people having a home set on a new lot. *Inferred from the trade.*
- They arrive on a phone, usually from a Facebook link, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- The owner, Joshua Paradis, sees it first: it has to read as his business, done better than he imagined.

## Purpose
Give the business a web presence where it has none (no site, no Maps listing, no reviews) and turn a visit into a quote request by text or a phone call.

## Positioning
A working crew that does home sets and relevels, in any weather. Their own line: "Rain or shine we work all the time give us a call for your next set or relevel."

## Capabilities and constraints
- Phone: (580) 775-1897. Owner says "give us a call". Whether it takes texts is **unknown**, so the quote form hands off by text and always offers the call beside it.
- No email, no booking link, no hours, no prices, no reviews, no logo, no work photos.
- Service area **unknown and must not be stated** (580 area code is Oklahoma; the ad ran in a New Jersey contractor group). Page says "call for service area".
- Do not claim licensing, insurance or years in business.

## Brand commitments
- Name as written: J's Home Renovations & Relevels LLC.
- Voice: casual, working crew, "we". Promise in their own words: "Rain or shine, we work all the time."
- No logo: the name is set as a wordmark. Colors are chosen by the build (no brand colors exist).

## Evidence on hand
- One Facebook group post (2026-10-18) with the caption above and a 13s video filmed from a truck in the rain; a home on blocks is visible through the wet window. Too blurry and personal to publish.
- Owner's personal Facebook profile (the only social link).

## Principles
1. Phone first; the first screen shows what they do and how to reach them.
2. Only facts from the brief; every inference is labelled in HANDOFF.md.
3. Explain what a "set" and a "relevel" are in plain words, because customers may not know the trade terms.
